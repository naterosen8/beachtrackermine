      Data sources

      • OpenStreetMap: beaches, parking, amenities
      • NOAA: official tide predictions · Open-Meteo: weather, waves, water temperature
      • SB County Environmental Health: ocean water testing (hotline 805-681-4949)
      • National Weather Service: beach hazards (rip currents, high surf, coastal flooding, tsunami)
      • OSRM: driving times · LADOT: LA street meters

      Good to know

      • Crowd estimates are guesses; live reports beat them.
      • Tides are NOAA predictions for the nearest station; actual water levels vary with surf and weather.
      • Swim near lifeguards and check posted signs.
